## About HPM India

Established with a four-decade legacy of excellence, **HPM Chemicals & Fertilizers Limited** is a trusted leader in India's agricultural ecosystem. Guided by our motto **"Yielding Prosperity"**, we empower farmers and agricultural stakeholders by delivering world-class crop protection solutions, plant nutrition, and advanced soil enrichment technologies.

Through this organization (`@hpmindiaorg`), our technology team builds and maintains the digital infrastructure, web portals, admin dashboards, and service APIs that support HPM's nationwide operations and customer network.

## Our Core Agricultural Portfolio

- **Crop Protection (Insecticides & Fungicides):** Formulations safeguarding crops against sucking, chewing pests and damaging fungal infections.
- **Weed Management (Herbicides):** Selective and broad-spectrum solutions to protect yields and crop health.
- **Plant Nutrition & Fertilizers:** Specialized soil enrichers, mycorrhizal bio-fertilizers, and balanced micronutrients.
- **Plant Growth Regulators (PGR):** High-efficiency formulations to optimize plant vigor, flowering, and harvest quality.

## Technology & Engineering Focus

Our engineering team develops reliable, high-performance web and enterprise applications:

- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Next.js, Modern CSS & Tailwind CSS.
- **Backend & APIs:** RESTful APIs, Node.js, Express, microservices architecture.
- **Data & Security:** Role-based access control (RBAC), schema validation (Zod / Joi), secure authentication.
- **DevOps & Standards:** CI/CD with GitHub Actions, strict linting, and Git-flow branch workflows.

## Contribution & Development Guidelines

1. **Branch Naming:**
   - Feature branches: `feature/<feature-name>`
   - Bugfixes: `bugfix/<issue-name>`
   - Active development: `dev`
   - Production releases: `main`

2. **Commit Standards:**
   Follow [Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/) (e.g., `feat:`, `fix:`, `docs:`, `refactor:`).
